⚙️ Orchestration Setup

Three specialized LLMs — Claude-3.5-Sonnet for extraction, GPT-4o for risk analysis, and Gemini-1.5-Pro for strategy — run concurrently via Promise.all() parallelism, coordinated by a lightweight JavaScript orchestrator layer. Prompt routing is handled per-agent based on task type. Total human input: one paste + one click. Output: a structured multi-perspective legal analysis with cross-agent synthesis — achieving a high MoJo Score by maximizing AI output relative to human effort.
